# opencompany Trademark Policy

The opencompany source code is available under an open-source license, but that license does not
grant permission to use the opencompany name, logos, or other brand features (the "Marks") except as
described here or as required by applicable law. This policy is intended to let people accurately
refer to the project while preventing confusion about who provides or endorses a product or service.

## Uses That Do Not Require Permission

You may:

- truthfully state that software uses, integrates with, is compatible with, or is derived from
  opencompany;
- reproduce the unmodified software using its original name when you preserve its license and
  notices and do not imply a separate product or service is official;
- use the word mark in articles, presentations, reviews, tutorials, and other commentary about the
  project; and
- use the word mark for a community group or event when the name makes its independent status clear
  and the following notice appears prominently: "This is an independent community activity and is
  not affiliated with or endorsed by opencompany."

Referential use should use no more of a Mark than necessary and should not be more prominent than
your own name or brand.

## Uses That Require Written Permission

Written permission is required to:

- name or brand a fork, distribution, product, hosted service, company, domain, package, or
  application in a way that uses or is confusingly similar to the Marks;
- use an opencompany logo except to link to or identify the official project;
- imply that a third party, product, or service is official, certified, sponsored, or endorsed;
- use the Marks on merchandise or in fundraising; or
- alter a logo and use the result as a source identifier.

## Forks and Modified Distributions

You may describe a modified distribution as "based on opencompany" or similar. Unless you have
written permission, give the distribution its own distinctive name, remove official logos, and state
clearly that the distribution is modified and not provided or endorsed by opencompany. The software
license's attribution and notice requirements continue to apply.

## Attribution

When practical, use this notice:

> opencompany and the opencompany logo are trademarks of their respective owner. All other marks
> belong to their respective owners.

Do not use registration symbols unless the relevant Mark is registered in the applicable
jurisdiction.

## Questions and Permission Requests

Open a GitHub issue titled `Trademark permission request` with non-confidential context. A
maintainer will establish a private channel if the request requires confidential information. The
project may update this policy as its marks, ownership, and community practices evolve.

Except for the permissions stated above, no trademark rights are granted. Failure to enforce this
policy in one instance is not a waiver of the right to enforce it in another.
