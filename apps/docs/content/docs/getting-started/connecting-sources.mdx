---
title: Connecting sources
description: Connect provider accounts and separately choose what should feed Brain.
---

opencompany separates **connecting an account** from **using it as a Brain source**. That distinction
is important: authorizing a provider can make agent actions available without automatically copying
provider content into workspace knowledge.

## Connect an account

Open **Settings → Integrations**, choose the Workspace or Personal group, and select a provider. The
connection flow may use OAuth, an API key, a provider webhook, a CLI subscription login, or a
verified phone pairing, depending on the provider.

After the connection returns, check its displayed account identity and status. A provider can report
**Needs reauthorization** or **Sync failed** even though a saved connection row still exists.

## Choose what feeds Brain

Open the destination Brain and then **Sources**. For providers with selectable scope, choose the
specific material opencompany may ingest:

- Slack conversations;
- Linear teams and issue/comment events;
- GitHub repositories and issue or pull-request activity;
- Gmail event types and any ingestion instructions;
- Google Drive files or recursive folders;
- HubSpot or Attio CRM objects and events.

Connecting these accounts is not enough on its own: Brain ingestion starts only after a source is
configured and enabled. Meeting-note providers such as Jamie, Granola, and Fathom do not have a
second item picker; once connected and enabled for a Brain, completed meeting material can flow in.

## Agent permissions are separate

Agent actions have their own modes. **On** allows the action, **Ask** pauses for confirmation, and
**Off** removes it from later turns. A provider's OAuth grant is the outer limit; opencompany's action
mode can narrow what the agent may do within that grant.

Changing an action permission does not retroactively change Brain source selection, and disabling a
Brain source does not disconnect the provider account. Review both settings when you want to stop a
provider from being used.

Provider-specific scope and ingestion details are being documented under
[Integrations](/docs/integrations). Until a provider page is complete, treat the provider consent
screen and the scopes shown on the saved connection as authoritative.
