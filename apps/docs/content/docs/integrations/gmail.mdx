## What connecting does

Gmail is a personal OAuth connection. Current actions can read mail, create new plain-text drafts for
manual review, and send new plain-text email when the send action is approved.

## Scopes and permissions

Reading, draft creation, and sending are separate opencompany permissions even where Google groups
provider access under the same OAuth grant. Sending defaults to a confirmation boundary. The full
Google scope list and reconnect rules are coming soon; review the consent screen before authorizing.

## Brain ingestion

Choose the supported sent or received email events and any ingestion instructions in the destination
Brain's Sources settings. Connecting Gmail alone does not ingest the mailbox.
