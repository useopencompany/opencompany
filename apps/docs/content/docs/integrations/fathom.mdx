## What connecting does

Fathom connects with a personal API key from Fathom's user settings. opencompany uses the account to
discover completed meeting recordings and summaries.

## Scopes and permissions

This connection is for meeting ingestion rather than a general agent-action catalog. A complete key
setup and troubleshooting guide is coming soon.

## Brain ingestion

Fathom has no per-meeting picker. Once connected and enabled for a Brain, completed meeting material
can enter the durable ingestion path.
