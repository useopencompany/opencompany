## What connecting does

Granola connects with a personal API key created in Granola. opencompany polls the authorized
account for completed meeting summaries.

## Scopes and permissions

This connection does not expose a general agent-action catalog. A complete guide to creating,
rotating, and troubleshooting the Granola key is coming soon.

## Brain ingestion

Granola has no per-meeting picker. Once the account is connected and enabled for a Brain, completed
meeting summaries can enter the durable ingestion path.
