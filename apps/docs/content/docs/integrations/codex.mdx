## What connecting does

The personal Codex connection authorizes the Codex engine used by cloud coding Chats. A successful
connection is validated and stored for the signed-in person; raw tokens are encrypted and are never
shared as a workspace API key.

Workspace admins can also choose **Use for workspace** on their Codex integration. When enabled,
opencompany routes the exact `GPT-5.6 Sol` and `GPT-5.6 Terra` models in Chat, Tasks, and the Slack
bot through that admin's ChatGPT subscription. Other models continue through the metered model
gateway. Subscription-backed turns appear as **ChatGPT subscription** at `$0` in billing activity
and do not consume workspace credits.

The workspace setting is visible to all members but only admins can change it. Removing the
designated admin from the workspace, disconnecting their Codex account, or disabling the setting
removes the route automatically.

## Scopes and permissions

Codex uses its own subscription authentication flow. If the designated account expires, opencompany
shows a reconnect error and does not silently charge workspace credits through another provider.
Reconnect Codex in **Settings → Integrations**, or ask another workspace admin to connect and select
**Use for workspace**. A ChatGPT usage-limit error likewise stays on the subscription route; wait for
the limit to reset or switch to a model that is not subscription-routed.

Repository and secret access still comes from the separate tools and connections available to the
coding workspace.

## Brain ingestion

Codex is not a Brain source. A Codex Conversation can receive scoped Brain read and capture tools
when a Brain is pinned, but its transcript is not ingested automatically.
