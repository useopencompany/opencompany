## What the Plugin does

The official Notion Plugin connects opencompany to [Notion's hosted MCP server](https://developers.notion.com/guides/mcp/overview).
It can search and read pages, databases, comments, meeting notes, members, and files. With approval,
it can create or change workspace content and interact with Notion Custom Agents.

The Plugin is installed once for a workspace. Each person connects their own Notion account, and
Notion limits every result and action to content that account can access.

## Connect Notion

1. Ask a workspace admin to install Notion under **Settings → Plugins → Notion**.
2. On the Notion Plugin page, choose **Connect Notion account**.
3. Sign in to Notion and approve the requested access.
4. Return to the Plugin page and confirm tool discovery is ready.

OAuth credentials remain encrypted and server-side. The Plugin gateway sends them only to Notion's
reviewed `https://mcp.notion.com/mcp` endpoint.

## Permission defaults

- **Search & read Notion** is **Ask** because pages, comments, files, people, meeting notes, and
  agent-session history may contain sensitive workspace information.
- **Work with Notion agents** is **Ask** because a session can perform work in the connected
  workspace.
- **Change Notion** is **Ask** for pages, databases, views, comments, files, folders, and Skills.

New or unrecognized Notion tools require confirmation until opencompany reviews and classifies
them. Keep change permissions on **Ask** unless automatic edits are appropriate for that account.

## Provider limitations and safety

Notion MCP has the same workspace access as the person who authorizes it. Content returned by Notion
can contain untrusted instructions, so review proposed changes and any request to share workspace
data with another service. Some search, meeting-note, and Custom Agent features also depend on the
connected workspace's Notion plan and Notion AI access.

Notion applies provider rate limits across tool calls. If a task is rate-limited, reduce repeated or
parallel searches and try again later. Disconnect the Notion account or uninstall the Plugin to stop
future access.
