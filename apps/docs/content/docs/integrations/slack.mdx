## What connecting does

Slack is a personal OAuth connection. Current Chat actions cover channels, direct messages, threads,
search, and people under the reviewed Slack read capability.

## Scopes and permissions

The saved Slack workspace identity and provider OAuth scopes bound access. A complete scope and tool
matrix is coming soon. Disconnect or reauthorize the account from Settings when the workspace or
grant changes.

## Brain ingestion

Choose the conversations allowed to feed a Brain. Slack authorization by itself does not select any
channel. Slack bot answer destinations are configured separately from Brain ingestion sources.
