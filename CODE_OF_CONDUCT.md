# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want participation in the opencompany community to be safe, respectful, and productive for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat people with empathy and professional respect.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept correction in good faith.
- Respect privacy, consent, project boundaries, and maintainer decisions.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, trolling,
publication of another person's private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories and community spaces, and when someone officially
represents opencompany in public or private interactions.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [team@opencompany.cloud](mailto:team@opencompany.cloud). Do
not include incident details in a public issue. For conduct hosted on GitHub, you may also use
GitHub's content-reporting tools.

The maintainers will review reports as privately and impartially as practical. Depending on
the context and severity, consequences may include a private correction, warning, temporary limits
on participation, removal of content, or a permanent ban. Retaliation against a reporter or witness
is itself a violation.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
and its community-impact enforcement model.
